About Me

Design with intention, craft with care.

I'm Chetana Mathyam, a graphic designer who believes great design emerges from the intersection of deep research, cultural sensitivity, and meticulous craft. My work spans brand identities, editorial systems, and print design.

I'm drawn to projects that need a distinct visual voice — work that tells a story, connects with its audience, and holds its own over time.
